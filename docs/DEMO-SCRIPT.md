# Demo video script (2 to 3 minutes)

1. **Hook (10 s):** "PerpPilot AI helps you trade dreamDEX perps with reasons, not just signals." Show the dashboard.
2. **Wallet (15 s):** Connect MetaMask, show "Somnia Hideki / Chain 50383 verified" and the address.
3. **Market data (20 s):** Switch BTC, ETH, SOL. Point out best bid / ask, 24h volume, intervals, refresh.
4. **Signal (30 s):** Read "Why this signal": trend, momentum, volatility, levels, verdict, risk rating. Show the trade plan.
5. **Risk calculator (20 s):** Equity 1000, risk 1%, show size / leverage / margin. Click "Use in order form".
6. **Paper trading (30 s):** Open a paper trade from the signal, show live P&L, close it, show history and win rate.
7. **Real order (40 s):** Show the Trading account (smart wallet), place a limit order below the market, approve in MetaMask, show the order in the table and the transaction on the explorer, then cancel it.
8. **Close (10 s):** "Rule-based, explainable, wallet-signed, testnet only. Repo and feedback reports are linked."

If real orders are not yet working, replace step 7 with: show the pre-flight checks and the clear "no collateral" message, and say plainly what is pending.
